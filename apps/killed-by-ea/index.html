Scope and sources

How entries are chosen

This archive covers supported events through 22 October 2026. Each entry links to a source and an archived copy.

  • A game appears only when evidence shows that the specific project ended during development. A cancellation known from reporting is labelled “reported cancelled”. A move or redesign alone does not count.
  • A studio is marked merged when its team and work continued under a named operating successor. A studio that ceased and whose staff were laid off or scattered is marked closed. A rename alone does not count.
  • A project is a named, standalone product, platform, label, or initiative that ended. Rebrands, store removals, and server or online-service shutdowns of released games are outside this archive.
  • Dates keep the precision given by the source. Uncertain dates carry a note, and a date without a known year is grouped under “Date unknown”.
  • Summaries use neutral wording and describe past events. Entries make no current-status claims.
